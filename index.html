EQTRACK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12/9/2025

EQTRACK (“the App”) is designed to help users organize, track, and manage equipment through QR codes, barcodes, geolocation, and worksite management features. This Privacy Policy explains how EQTRACK collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Contact Information

  • Email address (used for account login, authentication, and support)

B. Location Information

  • Precise location when using geolocation features such as worksite check-ins, alerts, and equipment reminders.

C. User-Provided Equipment Data

  • Equipment names, serial numbers, descriptions, photos (optional), and other details you enter into the App.

D. Worksite or Physical Address Data

  • Locations you manually add for job sites, events, or equipment assignments.

E. Identifiers

  • User ID associated with your account
  • Device identifiers required for app functionality

F. Purchase Information

  • Purchase or subscription history if you choose to upgrade features within the App.

G. Other Technical or Log Data

  • System logs, check-in timestamps, and metadata generated through normal app usage.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for App Functionality, including:

  • Account authentication
  • Syncing and storing your equipment data and locations
  • Generating QR/barcodes for equipment tracking
  • Providing geolocation-based reminders and alerts
  • Saving check-in or check-out activity
  • Preventing fraud and maintaining security
  • Managing purchases, subscriptions, and restoring access

EQTRACK does not sell, share, or use your data for advertising, marketing, analytics, or tracking purposes.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Information may only be shared with:

  • Service providers required to operate the App (hosting, authentication, database storage)
  • Law enforcement if required by applicable law

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

5. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access the data we store
  • Delete your account and all stored information
  • Update your account details

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

EQTRACK is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
